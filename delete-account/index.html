會刪除哪些資料

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  • iPhone: Settings → Delete account
  • Android: Settings → Delete account

Can't open the app?

Email tw466376@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". Once we confirm it's you, we delete it within 7 days and let you know.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile and photo: deleted for good right away.
  • Your shared space (messages, photos, memories and so on): your partner sees it was disconnected and can export it for 30 days, then it is deleted for good.
  • Device tokens for notifications: deleted right away.

Apple subscriptions are cancelled separately in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

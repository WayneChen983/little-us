說明與客服

有任何問題、建議或想回報錯誤，請寫信到 tw466376@gmail.com，我們通常會在 2 個工作天內回覆。

怎麼跟另一半配對？

兩個人各自用 Apple 登入。一個人在配對畫面建立邀請碼傳給對方，另一個人輸入邀請碼就完成了。

怎麼取消 Couple Plus 訂閱？

打開 iPhone 的「設定」→ 點你的名字 →「訂閱項目」→ Little Us，就可以取消。取消後到期前都還能使用。

怎麼刪除帳號？

在 App 裡打開「設定」→ 最下面的「刪除帳號」。你的個人資料會立即刪除。

通話接不通？

請確認兩支手機都已允許麥克風（視訊還需要相機）權限，並更新到最新版本。仍然有問題請來信，附上當時雙方使用的網路（Wi‑Fi 或行動網路）。

Help & feedback

Questions, ideas or a bug? Email tw466376@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

How do we pair?

Each of you signs in with Apple. One of you creates an invite code on the pairing screen and sends it; the other enters it.

How do I cancel Couple Plus?

Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Little Us. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Delete account at the bottom. Your personal data is deleted right away.

A call won't connect?

Make sure both phones allow the microphone (and the camera for video) and run the latest version. If it still fails, email us and tell us which networks you were both on.

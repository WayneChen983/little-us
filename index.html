常見問題Questions

怎麼跟另一半配對？

在配對畫面建立邀請連結，傳給另一半；對方點一下就能加入你們的小天地。

另一半還沒準備好，可以先自己用嗎？

可以。在配對畫面點「先自己用」，就能先記錄心情和在一起的天數。之後隨時都能邀請另一半，原本的東西都會保留，變成你們兩個人的。

換手機的話，資料會不見嗎？

不會。你們的訊息、照片、回憶和心情都存在伺服器上，在新手機登入同一個帳號，就會全部回來。

免費版和 Couple Plus 有什麼不同？

每個功能都可以免費使用。免費版有 1,000 筆回憶、聊天照片保留 1 年、每天 10 張今日一拍、5 種主題、30 張自製貼圖，視訊每次 1 小時。

Couple Plus 一人購買，兩人共享：HD 無時限視訊、無限回憶、聊天照片永久保留、全部主題、植物、特效與信紙、100 張自製貼圖和原畫質照片。月繳 NT$99、年繳 NT$990，或終生 NT$1,990。

怎麼取消 Couple Plus 訂閱？

打開 iPhone 的「設定」→ 點你的名字 →「訂閱項目」→ Little Us，就可以取消。取消後到期前都還能使用。

怎麼刪除帳號？

在 App 裡打開「設定」→ 最下面的「刪除帳號」。你有 30 天可以再次登入取消；之後個人資料會永久刪除，共同回憶會留給另一半。你也可以先在 App 內匯出資料。沒辦法打開 App，或只想刪除部分資料，請看刪除帳號或資料。

通話接不通？

請確認兩支手機都已允許麥克風（視訊還需要相機）權限，並更新到最新版本。仍然有問題請來信，附上當時雙方使用的網路（Wi‑Fi 或行動網路）。

How do we pair?

Create an invite link on the pairing screen and send it to your partner. They tap once to join your tiny world.

My partner isn't ready yet. Can I start on my own?

Yes. Tap "Start on my own for now" on the pairing screen to keep your moods and your days together. Invite your partner anytime: everything stays and becomes yours together.

Will I lose anything if I change phones?

No. Your messages, photos, memories and moods live on our servers: sign in to the same account on the new phone and it's all there.

What’s the difference between free and Couple Plus?

Every feature is free to use. Free includes 1,000 memories, chat photos kept for one year, 10 Snaps a day, five themes, 30 custom stickers, and one hour per video call.

One Couple Plus purchase is shared by both of you, adding unlimited HD video, unlimited memories, chat photos kept forever, every theme, plant, effect and paper, 100 custom stickers, and original-quality photos. It’s NT$99 monthly, NT$990 yearly, or NT$1,990 lifetime.

How do I cancel Couple Plus?

Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Little Us. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Delete account at the bottom. You have 30 days to sign back in and change your mind; after that your personal data is deleted for good, while your partner keeps your shared memories. You can also export your data first. Can't open the app, or only want to delete some data? See Delete your account or data.

A call won't connect?

Make sure both phones allow the microphone (and the camera for video) and run the latest version. If it still fails, email us and tell us which networks you were both on.

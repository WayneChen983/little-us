Little Us（以下稱「我們」）是一個只屬於兩個人的情侶 App。我們把你們的隱私放在第一位：不賣資料、不放廣告、不跨 App 追蹤你。

我們收集哪些資料

  • 帳號：使用 Apple 登入時，Apple 提供的帳號識別碼、電子郵件（可能是 Apple 的隱藏信箱）與名字。
  • 個人資料：你填寫的暱稱、生日、交往紀念日、性別、選擇的小動物與大頭照。
  • 你們的內容：訊息、照片、手寫紙條、貼圖、心情、回憶與照片的拍攝地點（用於回憶地圖）、共同清單、時光膠囊、每日一問的回答；以及你選擇開啟時的生理期資料。
  • 通話：語音與視訊通話以點對點加密傳輸；無法直連時會經由 Cloudflare 的中繼伺服器加密轉送。我們不錄音、不錄影，也不保存通話內容，只保存通話紀錄（時間與長度）。
  • 心跳：只有在你按下「傳送心跳」時，App 才會從 Apple「健康」讀取當下的心率，把數字傳給另一半，不會另外保存。
  • 裝置與推播：推播通知所需的裝置代碼。
  • 購買：訂閱透過 Apple 處理，我們只會收到訂閱狀態與到期日，不會取得你的付款資訊。
  • 使用紀錄：每天是否開啟過 App（只記日期），用來了解配對與留存狀況。

誰看得到

你們空間裡的內容只有你和配對的另一半看得到。這是由伺服器的資料庫權限強制執行，不只是 App 畫面上的限制。我們不會把你的資料賣給任何人，也不會用於廣告。

服務供應商

我們使用以下服務來提供 App：Supabase（資料庫、檔案儲存、登入與即時連線）、Apple（登入、推播通知與付款）、Cloudflare（通話中繼）。這些供應商只會依我們的指示處理資料。

資料保存

  • 免費方案的聊天照片保存 90 天；存進「回憶」的照片會一直保留。
  • 解除配對後，共享空間會保留 30 天讓你們匯出，之後永久刪除。
  • 刪除帳號後，你的個人資料會立即刪除。

你的權利

你可以在 App 中匯出聊天與回憶、修改個人資料，或在「設定 → 刪除帳號」刪除帳號。其他請求請來信 tw466376@gmail.com。

兒童

Little Us 不適合 13 歲以下兒童使用，我們也不會刻意收集兒童的資料。

政策變更

如果本政策有重要變更，我們會在 App 內或本頁通知。

Little Us ("we") is a private app for couples. Your privacy comes first: we don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple, the account identifier, email (possibly Apple's private relay address) and name Apple shares.
  • Profile: the nickname, birthday, anniversary, gender, animal and profile photo you enter.
  • Your content: messages, photos, handwritten notes, stickers, moods, memories and where photos were taken (for the memory map), shared lists, time capsules, daily question answers, and cycle data if you turn it on.
  • Calls: voice and video calls are encrypted and peer to peer; when a direct connection isn't possible they are relayed, still encrypted, through Cloudflare. We never record or store calls, only a call log (time and length).
  • Heartbeat: only when you tap "send your heartbeat" does the app read your current heart rate from Apple Health and send the number to your partner; it isn't stored separately.
  • Device and notifications: the device token needed for push notifications.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Apple; we only receive the subscription status and expiry, never your payment details.
  • Usage: whether you opened the app on a given day (the date only), to understand pairing and retention.

Who can see it

What's in your space is visible only to you and your partner. The server's database permissions enforce this, not just the app. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in and realtime), Apple (sign-in, notifications and payments) and Cloudflare (call relay). They process data only on our instructions.

Retention

  • On the free plan, chat photos are kept for 90 days; photos saved to Memories are kept.
  • After you disconnect, the shared space is kept for 30 days so you can export it, then deleted for good.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted right away.

Your choices

You can export chats and memories in the app, edit your profile, or delete your account in Settings → Delete account. For anything else, email tw466376@gmail.com.

Children

Little Us isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we'll let you know in the app or on this page.
